# Code Of Conduct

cms-lab contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, specific, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without personal attacks.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Keep feedback focused on code, docs, tests, product behavior, or user impact.
- Respect maintainers' scope decisions.
- Credit prior work and report uncertainty clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or targeted insults.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic arguments.
- Pressuring maintainers to ship unverified or misleading claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or decline contributions that violate this code. Serious concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainer.
